Ordering at a café
Barista · Turn 3 / ~10
T
Hi there! What can I get started for you today?
起手常见句式：What can I get started for you? / What'll it be? 都比 What do you want 自然得多。
I would like a large latte, please.
T
Perfect. Whole milk, oat, or something else?
咖啡师会先确认牛奶——记住 oat（燕麦）/ almond（杏仁）/ whole（全脂）/ skim（脱脂）。
Oat milk. And can I get a extra shot?
✎ a → an extra shot
T
You got it. That's $6.25. Cash or card?
